THE ENTITY'S DESCENT

A Story of Survival and Sacrifice

"The Entity doesn't want you to be perfect. It just wants to see how much pain you can take."

Time to finish: 15–25 Hours (Over 30 Hours if you dare to try the hardest modes)

Welcome to the fog! This is a massive, long-form challenge where every single choice matters. Your ultimate goal? Win a game with every single Killer. But there is a twist: if you lose a match, the game isn't over. Instead, things just get darker and harder. Here is how your story unfolds.

Step 1: Choose Your Weapons

Before your Killer steps into the trial, you have to build their loadout. Think of the perks in Dead by Daylight as 8 different "schools of magic" (we call them Disciplines). You are only allowed to pick four different schools. You cannot pick the same one twice! Once you have your four schools, choose exactly one perk from each.

Step 2: The Downward Spiral

You start your first game feeling powerful with all 4 of your chosen perks. If you win, congratulations! That Killer is completely finished. But if you lose? You begin The Descent. The Entity demands a sacrifice. You must throw away one of your perks forever. You get to choose which one to lose, but you are now weaker. If you lose again, you lose another perk. And another.

The Last Hope: The Perkless Lifeline

What happens when you lose all your perks? The Entity gives you one final chance to prove yourself. You must play a game with no perks at all.

If you somehow survive and win this test, you get to climb back up! You earn back 1 perk and get to keep trying.

But if you lose this final test? The Entity gets angry. Those 4 perks you originally picked are now banned forever for that specific Killer. You have to start their story all over again by drafting 4 brand new perks.

The True Nightmare: Ascension Tiers

Did you manage to win a game with every single Killer? Amazing! But the story isn't over. Now, you unlock the endgame. These are terrifying new rules that change everything.

I

Weakened Tools

You are no longer allowed to use strong items. Only the weakest Brown and Yellow add-ons are allowed.

II

The Entity's Choice

Before you even pick your perks, the Entity randomly bans two whole Disciplines. You have to make do with what's left.

III

A Fast Fall

The punishment for losing is much faster. If you lose once, you drop straight down to 2 perks. Lose again, and you are immediately at zero.

IV

Perfect or Nothing

A normal win isn't good enough anymore. If even one survivor escapes (through the gates or the hatch), it counts as a loss. You must kill all four.
